# Stockroom Warranty Policy

**Document ID:** POL-WAR-002 · **Effective:** 1 March 2026 · **Owner:** Customer Operations

## 1. Coverage periods

| Category | Warranty period |
|---|---|
| Workshop tools (powered, e.g. cordless drills) | **24 months** |
| Workshop tools (hand tools) | **12 months** |
| Electronics accessories | **12 months** |
| Safety equipment | **6 months**, or the manufacturer's stated service life if shorter |
| Office supplies, packaging, cleaning products | no warranty beyond the returns window |

The warranty period starts on the delivery date shown on the order.

## 2. What is covered

Defects in materials or workmanship under normal use. Stockroom repairs, replaces or refunds at its
discretion.

## 3. What is not covered

- Wear items: drill bits, blades, batteries after 6 months, gloves, cloths.
- Damage from misuse, modification, or use outside the manufacturer's rating.
- Consumables such as toner and cleaning chemicals.

## 4. Making a claim

Quote the order number and SKU, describe the fault, and include photos where possible. Customer
Operations responds within **2 business days**. Supplier batch numbers are kept for 12 months to support
claims.
