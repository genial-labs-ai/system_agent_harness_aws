# Stockroom Returns Policy

**Document ID:** POL-RET-004 · **Effective:** 1 March 2026 · **Owner:** Customer Operations

## 1. Return window

Unused items in their original packaging may be returned within **30 days** of the delivery date for a
full refund to the original payment method. Items returned between **31 and 60 days** after delivery are
eligible for **store credit only**. No returns are accepted after 60 days.

## 2. Non-returnable items

The following cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or defective:

- Opened consumables (cleaning chemicals, hand sanitiser, printer toner once the seal is broken).
- Personal protective equipment that has been worn (gloves, ear defenders, steel toe boots).
- Custom-printed labels and custom-cut packaging.

## 3. Damaged or defective deliveries

Report damage within **5 business days** of delivery with a photograph. Stockroom arranges collection at
no cost and ships a replacement or issues a refund, at the customer's choice.

## 4. Restocking fee

A **15% restocking fee** applies to returned workshop tools and electronics with a unit price above
**$100** when the return is not due to a Stockroom error. Office supplies, packaging and cleaning products
carry no restocking fee.

## 5. How to start a return

1. Quote the order number (format `ORD-####`).
2. State the SKU and quantity being returned.
3. Customer Operations issues a Returns Authorisation (RA) number within one business day.
4. Refunds are processed within **7 business days** of the warehouse receiving the goods.
