# Stockroom Shipping Policy

**Document ID:** POL-SHP-007 · **Effective:** 1 March 2026 · **Owner:** Logistics

## 1. Service levels

| Service | Cut-off | Transit time | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | 14:00 local | 2–3 business days | **$8.50** per order, **free on orders over $250** |
| Express | 12:00 local | next business day | **$19.00** per order |
| Pallet freight | 10:00 local | 3–5 business days | quoted per consignment |

Orders placed after the cut-off are dispatched on the next business day. Business days are Monday to
Friday, excluding public holidays at the dispatching warehouse.

## 2. Carriers and tracking

Parcels ship with DPD, UPS, DHL or Parcelforce. A tracking number is issued when the parcel leaves the
warehouse and is visible on the order record (`tracking_number`). Tracking numbers start with the first
two letters of the carrier name (for example `DP` for DPD).

## 3. Backorders and split shipments

If any line is out of stock the order status becomes **backordered**. In-stock lines ship immediately
at no extra cost; the remaining lines ship when stock arrives, normally within the supplier lead time
listed in the supplier lead-time schedule. Customers can cancel the backordered lines at any time before
dispatch.

## 4. Delivery problems

- **Late delivery:** if a Standard parcel has not arrived within 5 business days of dispatch, contact
  Customer Operations and quote the tracking number.
- **Lost parcel:** carriers treat a parcel as lost after **10 business days** without a scan; Stockroom
  then reships or refunds.
- **Wrong address:** address changes are possible until the parcel is scanned by the carrier.

## 5. International

Stockroom ships to the UK, EU and US only. Duties and import taxes outside the UK are the customer's
responsibility.
